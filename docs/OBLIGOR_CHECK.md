## Notes

1. **Frontier Term Loan B-1 is not an "Initial Term Loan".** The memo's A.1 row describes the Frontier term loan as the "Initial Term Loans". The held tranche (N-PORT: "2017 Term Loan B1", 35906EAJ9) was made under Increase Joinder No. 1 of June 15, 2017: "the Borrower has notified the Administrative Agent that it is requesting Incremental Term Loan Commitments pursuant to Section 2.21(a) of the Credit Agreement in the form of Term B-1 Loans". The joinder attaches the amended agreement. In it, §7.01(i) ("the Borrower or any of its Principal Subsidiaries shall (i) voluntarily commence any proceeding …") and the automatic-acceleration tail ("in case of any event with respect to the Borrower described in clause (h) or (i) of this Section 7.01 … shall automatically become due and payable") have the same wording the memo quotes. The memo's conclusion (AUTOMATIC) holds for B-1. Only the tranche label should change. I did not open the later Frontier amendments (8-Ks of 2018-01-25, 2018-07-05, 2019-03-18), which full-text search also returns for "Term B-1".
2. **Every quoted obligor except one is named as a debtor in a filed EDGAR document.** Each of these obligors is the registrant itself, or is named as "a Debtor" in a filed DIP agreement: Frontier Communications Corp, Intelsat Jackson Holdings S.A., Chesapeake Energy Corp and Whiting Petroleum Corp. The parent-only question does not arise for these four. For Intelsat, the registrant (Intelsat S.A.) is not the obligor. The petition 8-K says only "certain of its subsidiaries", so I used the June 17, 2020 DIP agreement, which names Jackson as "a Debtor and Debtor in Possession".
3. **Endo Luxembourg Finance Company I S.à r.l.: NOT VERIFIED by name on EDGAR.** What I tried:
   - (a) The petition 8-K and its interim cash-collateral order. These cover the debtors generically and point to the Kroll claims-agent site for the list: "a complete list of the debtor entities … is not provided herein".
   - (b) The RSA in the same 8-K. It defines "Debtors" as "Endo International plc ("Parent") and each of its undersigned subsidiaries", but the filed signature pages are blank ("[Debtor] By: Name: Title:").
   - (c) The Fourth Amended Plan (8-K EX-2.1, 2024-03-25; also Endo, Inc. S-1 EX-2.1). It defines "Debtors" as "Endo International plc and its affiliated debtors in the Chapter 11 Cases" and has no list.
   - (d) The April 14, 2024 purchase and sale agreement (8-K EX-2.1, 2024-04-18). It lists Endo Luxembourg Finance Company I among the "Luxembourg Sellers", but "Sellers" is not the same set as the debtors. Its recital says only "Seller Parent and certain of the other Endo Companies filed voluntary petitions".
   - (e) EDGAR full-text search for "Endo Luxembourg Finance Company I" together with "Debtors" (26 hits). None names it as a debtor.

   The strongest evidence is the 10-K for 2022, Note 23 (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1593034/000159303423000007/endp-20221231.htm): "the Debtors only, which include Endo International plc and most of its wholly-owned subsidiaries, except for its Indian subsidiaries and certain subsidiaries associated with the Company's former Astora business". The Luxembourg finance company is neither Indian nor Astora-related, so it is very likely a debtor. This is an inference, not a quoted name. **It does not affect the conclusion.** Both Endo instruments it is party to accelerate automatically on a filing by the Parent (6.125% notes §6.02) or by "Parent or any Borrower" (2021 credit agreement §7.01). Endo International plc is named as a filer.
4. **Endo has an Item 2.04, but in a separate 8-K.** Memo note 4 says the Endo petition 8-K has no Item 2.04. That is correct for 0001193125-22-222687. A second 8-K, filed 2022-08-19 (0001193125-22-225297, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1593034/000119312522225297/d389791d8k.htm), has Item 2.04: "The Filing constitutes an event of default that accelerated the Company's obligations under the following debt instruments …". The list covers the 2017/2021 Credit Agreement, the Par 7.500% notes indenture and the 6.125% notes indenture. It continues: "The Debt Instruments provide that as a result of the Chapter 11 Cases, the principal and interest due thereunder shall be immediately due and payable." So all five issuers made an Item 2.04-type statement, not four of five.
5. **Par 7.500% notes have the same structure as the Endo 6.125% notes.** The EoD is broad (Parent, Issuer or any Significant Subsidiary). Acceleration is automatic only for a Parent filing; any other filer's EoD needs a declaration. The Parent filed on the same date as Par, so the notes were automatically accelerated on 2022-08-16. The same cross-reference inconsistency appears: "6.01(7) and 6.01(8)" in one place versus "Sections 6.01(7) and 6.01(8)" in another. The trustee named in the 2019 indenture is Wells Fargo. Computershare is the trustee named in the 2022 filings.
6. **CUSIP matches.** Three held CUSIPs are printed in the exhibit's form of note: 45824TBA2 (Intelsat 8.5% 2024), 29280BAA3 (Endo 6.125% 2029) and 69888XAA7 (Par 7.5% 2027). The Frontier, Chesapeake and Whiting note exhibits use only placeholder CUSIP language, so the match rests on issuer, coupon and maturity. Loan CUSIPs (35906EAJ9, L5137LAF2, 16516FAH2, L2968EAE2) never appear in credit agreements; the match rests on borrower, agreement date and tranche name.
7. **Intelsat credit agreement.** As in memo note 2, I read only the 2013 conformed text. Later amendments were not opened. Debtor status of the borrower is confirmed either way.
